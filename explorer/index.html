Quince Embedding Explorer

106 mock Quince product descriptions, embedded with BERT-family encoders (plus a masked-set-model tuned space) and projected to 2D with UMAP. Click any dot to see its nearest neighbors by cosine similarity, or type a shopper query to embed it into the same space. Distances on the map are approximate; the neighbor list uses the full 768-dimension vectors.

Each of the 10 slots is filled greedily with the product that maximizes relevance × sim + margin × margin − diversity × (max sim to products already picked), with each term scaled to 0–1.

Average similarity between categories

Mean cosine similarity for every pair of products drawn from the two categories. Outlined cells are within-category. A good encoder makes the outlined cells clearly darker than the rest of their row.

How many natural groups?

Silhouette score for KMeans at each k (higher means tighter, better-separated clusters). The highlighted bar is the best k; the darker grey bar is k=5, the number of real categories.

Mock catalog data written for a case study, not pulled from Quince. Embeddings from bert-base-uncased (mean-pooled) and sentence-transformers/all-mpnet-base-v2. The set-model tuned space is MPNet passed through an adapter trained with a masked set model on taxonomy menus.
